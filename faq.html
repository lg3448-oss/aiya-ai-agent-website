Getting started

What does AI Pilot do?

We design, connect, and operate custom AI agents for recurring business work. We start with a task, build around your tools, and review the workflow with your team.

What kinds of work are a good fit?

Recurring tasks with clear inputs and outputs your team can review are a good starting point. Marketing, research, and content are examples; we assess the fit for your business together.

Do we need technical experience?

You can start with a business problem rather than a technical specification. Bring an example of the work, the tools you use, and what a useful result would look like.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

One repeated task and an example of the result you want. It also helps to know who does the work today, where the information lives, and who should review the output.

02

Tools & control

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

We usually start with the tools you already use. Connections depend on APIs, account permissions, and platform rules, so we confirm feasibility before agreeing the scope.

Can every tool shown on the website be connected?

The tool selection on this website is an example stack. A logo is not a promise of an available integration. We assess each connection against your accounts, access, and intended workflow.

Will the agent act without our approval?

We agree which actions need a reviewer and which can run automatically. Actions such as publishing or updating records follow the permissions and approval steps agreed for your workflow.

Are the website demos connected to real accounts?

No. They are interactive examples of a workflow. Selecting tools or exploring a demo does not connect an account, authorize access, or publish content.

03

Scope, costs & support

How are service fees and AI usage handled?

AI Pilot’s service is scoped separately from the AI credits and platform costs your business covers. We discuss both before work begins; the website does not quote a fixed price for every workflow.

How long does it take to get started?

Timing depends on the task, integrations, and approvals involved. After understanding the first workflow, we propose a scope and timeline for you to review.

What happens after the agent is built?

Our service includes operating, reviewing, and improving the agreed workflow with your team. We discuss the ongoing scope and how changes will be handled before work begins.

Can we start with one task and expand later?

Yes. Starting with one clearly defined task gives us a focused outcome to assess together. Additional workflows and connections can be scoped as your needs become clearer.
